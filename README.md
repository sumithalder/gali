# गली (Gali)

**A little piece of India — a lane of Old Bollywood.**

Gali is a single-scene, illustrated Indian street corner you explore rather than browse. There's no navigation, no pages, no landmarks to check off — just an ordinary gali (lane) at dusk, a chai stall, a barber shop, a sleeping dog, and a music player looping old Hindi film songs. You look around, click on things, and the street tells you a little about itself.

## What's in the scene

- **Discoverable hotspots** — hover over the chai shop, barber shop, kirana store, sleeping dog, or balcony to see a handwritten note/receipt, click to hear a short sound clip from that spot
- **Day and night** — the scene automatically crossfades between a day and a night illustration based on the real time of day (6am–6pm = day), with a manual toggle in the top bar to override it
- **The street sleeps too** — once it's night, only the chai stall stays "open" (click prompt + sound); everything else just shows a closed-for-the-night note
- **Ambient soundscape** — a looping street ambience plays quietly behind the music and ducks out of the way when a hotspot sound plays
- **Music player** — a YouTube-powered player loops a playlist of old Hindi film songs, with shuffle, scrubbing, and volume control
- **Mouse parallax, grain, dust motes** — small ambient motion to keep the illustration from feeling static
- **Live visitor count** — "___ have passed through this gali," a simple hit counter
- **Mobile-aware** — hotspots (and the hint to look for them) are hidden below 560px, since hover-driven discovery doesn't translate to touch
